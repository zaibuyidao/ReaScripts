ROM folder

The library scans this folder and its subfolders. Your choice is saved automatically.

Library

Off by default. Downloads and caches Libretro covers using the ROM’s game code; ROM files are never uploaded. Cached covers remain available when turned off. If no cover is found, the first letter of the game code is shown. Translations, ROM hacks and homebrew may not have matching covers.

Cover grid puts artwork first; Details shows covers, file sizes and play time; Compact list fits more games. You can also switch views above the library. Your choice is saved automatically.

Video

Built-in LCD effects. Your choice is saved automatically. Integer scaling is recommended; LCD3X looks best at 3× or higher. Shaders use their own texture sampling.

Audio

Audio is muted during fast-forward and resumes at 1× speed.

Keyboard controls

Click a key, then press its replacement. Press Esc to cancel.

BIOS (optional)

Leave blank to use the built-in BIOS implementation. Takes effect the next time you load a game.
